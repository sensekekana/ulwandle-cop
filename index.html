Our Story

We are a young, driven, and dynamic team of professionals with various skill sets and experience. We are a Level 1 BBBEE Contributor based in the city of Richards Bay, but we also have a satellite office in Johannesburg.

Our strength comes from our diverse team of experts, merging youth with experience including the use of modern technologies to conduct our work. Ulwandle Eco Marine has a passion for the environment with our foundation centred on sustainability and green practices at the core of everything that we do.

As a company, we are focused largely on four main sectors of the ocean economy: infrastructure, cold chain, export, and commercial diving. Our company continues to look for growth and collaborative opportunities within the maritime space, whilst also recruiting the best talent and cutting-edge innovative technologies.

Our commitment

We have the capability and capacity to handle the entire value chain of your cold chain needs. Our goal is to create long term value for the organisations that trust us with their challenges, their vision and most importantly with their budgets.
